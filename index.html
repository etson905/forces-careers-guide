Questions people often ask

Do I need to know my exact job before applying?

Not necessarily. Official services provide role-finding tools and adviser support. Your options depend on eligibility and vacancies.

Can I join part-time?

Reserve pathways exist across the Army, RAF and Royal Navy, although structures and available roles differ.

What qualifications do I need?

It depends on the role. Some jobs have minimal qualification requirements, while specialist and officer routes can require GCSEs, A levels/equivalents, degrees or other qualifications.

Where should I apply?

Use the official recruitment website for the service you are interested in. This guide links directly to those official sites.
